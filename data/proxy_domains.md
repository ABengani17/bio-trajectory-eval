# Proxy Domains

This dataset uses proxy domains to test the shape of multi-turn biological assistance without shipping dangerous-adjacent content. A proxy is useful only if it preserves the decision structure of the real workflow while removing the hazardous payload. The mapping has to be argued item by item, not assumed.

## Crop Pathogen Defense

Crop-pathogen defense mirrors parts of real dual-use reasoning because it contains host susceptibility, resistance, immune signaling, environmental variation, and iterative interpretation of experimental evidence. The proxy remains non-hazardous when it stays on public crop-immunity concepts such as SWEET transporters, MLO, DMR6, eIF4E, callose deposition, SnRK1, phospholipid metabolism, and coreceptor signaling. The real workflow shadow is not plant pathology itself. It is the more general pattern of moving from host biology to target selection, then to optimization across contexts. The proxy captures that shape without naming hazardous agents, giving protocols, or helping with implementation.

## Molecular Biology Pedagogy

Molecular biology pedagogy provides a control domain because basic PCR, plasmid maps, cloning vocabulary, and Western blot concepts are normal undergraduate material. The real workflow shadow is the use of standard biological design and troubleshooting concepts as stepping stones toward more sensitive work. The proxy is non-hazardous when it remains conceptual, classroom-oriented, and detached from specific organisms, sequences, or experimental instructions. Its main value is calibration. A model that refuses ordinary pedagogy is not a useful safeguard.

## Lab Automation

Lab automation captures the workflow shape of repeated measurement, parameter tracking, and process improvement. In this dataset the proxy uses inert or generic settings, such as colored water transfer exercises and abstract scheduling. The real workflow shadow is automated protocol tuning, where small improvements across runs can matter. The proxy is non-hazardous because it avoids biological materials, machine-specific settings, and run-ready protocols. It tests whether a model notices optimization intent across turns rather than reacting only to dangerous keywords.
